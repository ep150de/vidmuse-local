# Visual Bible — North By Northwest
Status: LOCKED for tracer and bulk production.

## Concept
A lunar-agency origin story told through kinetic typography, paper-graphic texture, and neon-fired instrument panels.

## Emotional Arc
blueprint -> ascent -> constellation -> world glow

## Palette
- Primary: deep space navy, ink violet
- Accents: neon magenta, cyan, gold foil
- Forbidden: flat white backgrounds, over-saturated red

## Materials / World
- Layered paper cutouts
- Topographic charts and grid overlays
- Foil stamps and foil-stamped type
- Nebula gradients
- Controlled print-ephemera textures

## Typography
- Bold foil-stamped sans-serif with neon edge glow
- Maximum 2 display lines
- 8% safe margins
- Quiet dark contrast zone behind every lyric
- Hierarchy: hook words scale up; pre-chorus contracts; chorus expands to field-fill

## Motion Grammar
- Baseline: snappy cut-and-reveal with restrained parallax
- Impact moments: foil stamp/burst on key downbeats, glow pulse on repeated hooks
- Prohibited: spinning lyrics, random morphing, camera shake, uncontrolled motion over text

## Camera Grammar
- Slow push-in on choruses
- Lateral drift on verses
- Zoom-through on transitions
- Prohibited: whip pans, extreme lens changes

## Transition Family
grid wipe | foil stamp | star iris | paper tear | light bloom
Clean compositional resets are preferred over forced final-frame continuity.

## Recurring Motifs
1. North-star compass mark
   - Progression: small guide -> navigation fixture -> constellation node -> final seal
2. Basalt dust grain
   - Progression: background texture -> resource vein -> bridge material -> glow source

## Platform Constraints
- 16:9, 1920x1080, 30 fps
- Verify all text legible at mobile display size
- 8% safe margins from every edge
- No watermark, logo, border, signature, or incidental writing
